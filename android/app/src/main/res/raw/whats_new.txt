# AnkiGo release notes, newest first. Bundled in the app (What's new on Settings > Updates, and the card after an update).
# "## <version>" starts a block; each "- " line is one change. "text @ Settings → X → Y" shows the setting's path under the text.
# "### Fixes" inside a block starts its bug fixes (folded under "Bugs & fixes").
# Only versions up to the installed one are shown, so the newest block can be written before the version number is raised.

## 0.25.0
- Battle messages (item found, daily gift, played fast …) slide in as a small card at the top left of the arena; the bar always shows the two moves.
- "Super effective!" and the other hit texts appear above the hit Pokémon's head, not on it.
- Battle conditions (confusion, Leech Seed, Reflect, weather …) show as small chips on the info cards with the turns left; tap one to see them all.
- Charge moves (Dig, Fly, Solar Beam …) show as a move tile with "Charging"; tap it to read what happens.
- Transform: Ditto takes the target's picture with a bright flash.
- Stat changes show as small green or red pills in the move details.
- The battle result is one light line; the unclear "Auto" is gone.
- Between battles the card area says "Battle over" until you tap Continue; the next card and its timer start then.
- Choose the HP bar colours: three steps, a smooth blend (the new default) or the Pokémon's type colour. @ Settings → Battle panel & windows → HP bar colours
- Bigger info boxes when the arena has room. @ Settings → Battle panel & windows → Bigger info boxes
- "Ask me" when you beat a better copy of a Pokémon you own: catch it, catch it and replace yours, or let it go. @ Settings → Game → When you beat a wild Pokémon
- Duplicates in the Box look like a small pile of cards, or show two Pokémon side by side. @ Settings → Look → Box
- NEW in the Box goes once you've seen it.
- Turn dots also show in picture-in-picture, split screen and the floating window.
- OK on a new card fills a grey turn dot: an answer with no power.
- Switching your Pokémon fills no dot; the wild Pokémon gets one free hit, and a card says so.
- Pokédex: a back-to-top arrow (also in the Box), a "Seen" filter, and a clear note when "All of" has too many types.
- Sharper battle backgrounds.
- Pictures for the 9 evolution items added in 0.23.0.
- When a learning card comes due, it shows at once instead of waiting for AnkiDroid's save.
### Fixes
- The wild info box stays next to its NEW mark.
- The OK button shows only once per new card.
- Switching your Pokémon on the Continue screen no longer starts the next battle.
- Opening AnkiGo from a widget no longer makes it vanish from Recents.
- Info bubbles in the battle close when you tap anywhere else or press Back.

## 0.24.0
- Pokémon details: one card shows what the Pokémon holds and its evolution line. The level sits next to Stats, and every title explains itself when tapped.
- "Wins" replaces "Defeats" in the details (a catch counts too); the achievement is now called Battles won.
- Matchups show arrow icons, and the multiplier without a pill.
- What's new: this list, and a card after each update. @ Settings → Updates
- Switching in a fight: a message says who comes in on your next move, and the switch's dot on the bar is mauve.
- Special and status moves make your Pokémon lean a little toward the target.
- Choose how often the battle Pokémon move when idle. @ Settings → Battle panel & windows → Pokémon movement
- Choose where the app icon and the Buddy widget open: Study (the default), Battle or the last screen. @ Settings → Widgets → Open AnkiGo on
- No boxes behind the battle panel's chips and the small buttons.
- Pokédex and Box filters are kept; the quick chips (Wishlist, the new Favourites, Shiny, In team) turn on and off in place.
- Stats: the chart remembers its choice, six rarely used charts are gone, and the streak calendar shows all seven day letters.
- Item card titles are in your accent colour; evolution items don't name Pokémon you haven't caught.
- Money shows a coin icon, or the word "coins", everywhere.
- "Save answers for cards that aren't first" moved to the Study page. @ Settings → Study → Advanced
- The Diagnostics log box is smaller.
### Fixes
- Editing a card's note, or moving it to a deck inside the one you study, keeps the answer side.
- No more lag while AnkiDroid saves an answer: the next card opens at once, and you can show its answer and answer it right away.
- Your Pokémon's status goes at once when you win, and it takes no burn or poison damage after the wild one faints.
- The Battle view shows one Continue button.
- The money in the Shop and on the Pokémon tab no longer jumps from 0 when the page opens.

## 0.23.0
- EVs and friendship now grow with every win, also when you catch a Pokémon. Friendship stops at 255; the new Soothe Bell gives ×1.5.
- Abilities work in battle, for your Pokémon and the wild ones: Intimidate, Static, Levitate, Sturdy and about 200 more. The details say what each one does.
- Moves that did nothing now work: Protect, Substitute, Reflect, Leech Seed, Rest, Belly Drum, Haze, Yawn, Taunt, Encore, Transform, Metronome and many more.
- Weather and terrain last 5 turns and change damage, healing and some moves.
- Flinching, confusion, binding moves, Outrage-style locks, crash damage, and moves whose power depends on the fight (Low Kick, Reversal, Return …).
- Charge moves (Solar Beam, Dig, Fly …) take one turn to charge. Hyper Beam and Giga Impact need a turn to recharge.
- Full Restore also cures your Main's status in a fight. 9 missing evolution items can now be found or bought.
- New Moves and effects page in Help.
### Fixes
- Smart move choice no longer wastes a turn on Counter, Protect and similar moves when they would do nothing.
- Shedinja has 1 HP, as in the main games.

## 0.22.2
### Fixes
- Card styles: when you change a note type's styling or templates in AnkiDroid, the next card in AnkiGo uses the new look.
- Flagging a card no longer sends it back to the question side.

## 0.22.1
### Fixes
- Coming back to the app: each tab starts at its main page, and screens opened from a tab (Diagnostics, Updates, Help …) are closed. A running study session or battle is never reset.
- Stats tab: tapping it while an achievement's details are open closes them.

## 0.22.0
- Team: each Pokémon has a ⋯ button for its options. Make main during a fight waits for the current card.
- Box and Pokédex: one filter row with a funnel button and quick chips. New type filter (Any of, All of, Only) inside Sort and filter.
- Pokémon size in lists. @ Settings → Look → Pokémon size in lists
- The Can evolve mark is a green double chevron, also on Team rows.
- Read more: a Bulbapedia link in the Pokémon details.
- Tapping the tab you are on goes back to its main page.
- Diagnostics: the page scrolls, groups fold, and the log has a full-screen view.
### Fixes
- The status icon goes away when the fight ends; status damage shows in its colour.
- Download everything: the cries of Pikachu, Meltan and Melmetal now download, and the log names each file that fails.

## 0.21.0
- Tapping an item in the Shop or the Bag opens a window with its text and buttons.
- Caught Pokémon move in the Pokédex and the Box. @ Settings → Look → Animate Pokémon in lists
- Download everything: one button gets all pictures, animations, item icons, backgrounds and cries.
- View settings has its own full screen with a live battle preview.
### Fixes
- Newer Pokémon (gen 6 and up) are no longer drawn blurry.
- Pokédex types: the Ground chip is back in place, and a new Any of / All of switch.

## 0.20.0
- Every item has a short and a full description. Evolution items list their Pokémon, TMs show power, accuracy and who can learn them.
- Shop and Bag: item cards open in place. Bag items no longer act on a tap: open the card, then Use, Give, Throw or Teach.
- Trade balls in the Bag: 5 Poké Balls for 1 Great Ball, 3 Great Balls for 1 Ultra Ball.
- Daily gift: reaching the daily goal gives one item instead of money; better gifts with a longer streak.
- Master Ball: one for the top Best streak tier (365 days); never sold.
- New app icon, coin icon and five item icons.
- New cards: one OK button, off by default. @ Settings → Study → New cards: one OK button
- Deck finished: the last battle plays out, then a Finish deck button.
- Money: each win or answer pays an exact share.

### Fixes
- Battle: info boxes no longer hide under the frame, XP bars fill across a level-up, and "can't move" says why (asleep, frozen, paralyzed).
